# AutoMinder Market R&D - 2026-08-23

## Executive Verdict

AutoMinder should not launch as a "vehicle health score" or AI mechanic app. The market is already crowded with broad vehicle logbooks, fuel trackers, reminders, cloud sync, fleet dashboards, and service-history products. AutoMinder's strongest launch lane is narrower and more defensible:

**A truthful, offline-first maintenance companion that tells a driver what needs attention, why, and what to log next.**

The product should win on trust, clarity, and low-friction logging before it claims prediction, diagnostics, AI, fleet scoring, cloud sync, or quote savings.

## Competitive Pattern

Competitors converge on the same basics:

- Service reminders by date and/or odometer.
- Fuel and expense tracking.
- Reports, charts, and export.
- Multiple vehicles.
- Backup or sync.
- Service history as resale proof.

The gap is not "another logbook." The gap is confidence: users do not want a dashboard full of numbers; they want to know whether something is due, why it is due, and what action is safe to take next.

## Launch Positioning

Recommended launch promise:

> Know what maintenance needs attention, what is coming up, and what you have already done - without creating an account.

Recommended pillars:

- Maintenance attention: overdue, due soon, upcoming, snoozed, completed, unknown.
- Evidence-based reminders: date, odometer, last service, and interval.
- Local recordkeeping: vehicles, services, mileage, fuel, and costs.
- Resale-ready history: CSV/shareable record based on user-entered data.
- Privacy-first posture: no account requirement; do not overclaim "no data collection" while Firebase, AdMob, Billing, and backups exist.

Do not claim at launch:

- Fleet Health Score or any 0-100 health score.
- The app "knows" driving patterns unless the algorithm and evidence are audited.
- Cloud backup/sync as a Pro feature unless a real account-backed sync product exists.
- PDF export unless it exists and is tested.
- A 7-day free trial unless Play Console products prove it.
- Quote Auditor, Counter Mode, AI mechanic, OCR, VIN lookup, recalls, diagnostics, OBD, family sharing, or fuel prediction unless explicitly in release scope and tested.

## Product Strategy

For v1.0, ship the trustworthy core:

- Reminders that are honest about missing mileage and stale data.
- Service and fuel logs that are fast enough to use at the pump or counter.
- A Home surface that answers: "What needs attention?" and "What should I do next?"
- Export that says "logged records," not "certified truth."
- Pro that is represented by actual Play products and actual unlocked surfaces.

For v1.1, the best market wedge remains a rules-first quote review flow, but it must be conservative:

- "Questions to ask" beats "this is a scam."
- Show confidence and source fields.
- No OCR or AI claims until evidence exists.
- Save approved work back to service history.

## Release Plan Impact

Immediate P0 work:

- Migrate Play Billing 7.1.1 to 9.1.0 before Play submission.
- Remove health-score, cloud-sync, unsupported prediction, PDF, and trial claims from Play copy and app strings.
- Decide whether monthly/yearly/lifetime products are active catalog or whether v1.0 is lifetime-only.
- Verify Data Safety and privacy policy against actual SDK behavior.

P1 work:

- Finish U1 maintenance-truth language across Home, Vehicle Detail, widget, strings, and metadata.
- Keep Baseline Profile generation as a performance follow-up unless release timing allows device evidence.
- Use Graphify for targeted architectural tracing, not broad product direction.
