# Top 100 Global Brand Name Database & Scoring Matrix
**Evaluated by:** Brand Strategy, Trademark Legal, ASO, and Automotive Marketing Panel  
**Scoring Criteria (10 Points Each, Max 70 Points Total):**
* **M (Memorability):** Stickiness on first exposure.
* **P (Pronunciation):** Phonetic ease across global languages.
* **G (Global Scalability):** Comprehension in US, Europe, Asia, Latin America.
* **T (Trademark Defensibility):** Freedom to operate, distinctiveness under Nice Class 9.
* **A (ASO Capability):** Synergy with 30-char Play Store title limits.
* **Pr (Premium Feeling):** Perceived luxury / willingness to pay $29–$79.
* **Ac (Automotive Connection):** Resonance with vehicles, mobility, mechanics.
