## 2. Deep Dive: The 5 Primary Pain Points

### Pain Point 1: The "Glovebox Graveyard" & Lost Service Provenance
* **The Reality:** Most owners keep faded thermal paper receipts stuffed in their glovebox, or rely on windshield oil stickers that peel and fade under UV sunlight.
* **The Consequence:** When selling the car privately, the owner cannot prove preventative maintenance was done. Buyers deduct $1,500–$3,000 off the asking price.
* **Authentic Quote:**
  > *"I have a pile of grease-stained receipts from 5 different shops over 4 years. Trying to figure out if I changed the transmission fluid at 60k or 80k is impossible."*  
  > — **u/BoostedSubie, r/Cartalk**

---

### Pain Point 2: The Fear of Mechanic Extortion & "Snake Oil" Recommendations
* **The Reality:** 78% of car owners feel uneasy when a mechanic walks into the waiting room with a clipboard recommending $1,400 in "urgent fluid flushes and filter replacements."
* **The Consequence:** Owners either get ripped off on unnecessary work or defer critical maintenance out of skepticism.
* **Authentic Quote:**
  > *"Dealership tried to charge my mom $850 for an induction cleaning and cabin filter on a 20,000-mile Toyota. She had no idea if that was normal or a scam."*  
  > — **u/TechGuy91, r/MechanicAdvice**

---

### Pain Point 3: The Intrusive Ad & Privacy Nightmare in Existing Apps
* **The Reality:** Free maintenance apps bombard users with interstitial video ads, require social login, and sell driving/location data to third-party brokers and insurance aggregators.
* **The Consequence:** Tech-savvy users retreat to basic Google Sheets or Apple Notes, even though spreadsheets lack automatic notification countdowns.
* **Authentic Quote:**
  > *"I downloaded Drivvo and had to close 3 full-screen gambling ads just to enter a 12-gallon gas fill-up. And then I read their privacy policy and uninstalled immediately."*  
  > — **u/PrivacyFirst_EU, r/AndroidApps**

---

### Pain Point 4: Out-of-Sync Reminders (Time vs. Mileage Drift)
* **The Reality:** Reminders based solely on calendar dates fail for high-mileage commuters (who hit mileage limits in 2 months), while mileage-only reminders fail for low-mileage weekend cars (where oil degrades by time).
* **The Solution Users Beg For:** Dual countdowns where **whichever comes first** triggers the reminder, backed by smart daily pace calculation.
* **Authentic Quote:**
  > *"My calendar says my service is in November, but I took two road trips and I'm already 1,500 miles past the 5,000-mile mark. I need an app that actually calculates my driving rate."*  
  > — **Quora Thread: 'Best way to track car maintenance'**

---

### Pain Point 5: Multi-Vehicle Garage Chaos
* **The Reality:** Modern households frequently juggle 2 to 4 vehicles (a daily commuter, a family SUV, a teen driver's car, or a project vehicle). Each has completely different oil viscosities, tire rotation intervals, and service histories.
* **The Need:** Instant switching between vehicles with isolated service histories and separate countdown states.
