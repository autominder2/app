## 1. Google Play Keyword Taxonomy & Search Intent

On Google Play, search intent is split into three primary buckets:
1. **Utility & Problem Solvers (High Conversion, High Volume):** Users with an immediate task ("oil change reminder", "car maintenance tracker").
2. **Expense & Telemetry Trackers (High LTV, Medium Volume):** Power users tracking fuel economy, trip mileage, and tax deductions ("fuel log", "mpg tracker").
3. **Vehicle Care & Resale Guardians (High Willingness-to-Pay):** Car enthusiasts and smart owners who want complete ownership records ("car care", "service history", "vehicle garage").

## 3. Google Play Metadata Optimization Formulas

Google Play limits:
* **App Title:** Max 30 characters (Strict gate. Every character is high-value real estate).
* **Short Description:** Max 80 characters (Visible on listing before expansion).
* **Long Description:** Up to 4,000 characters (Indexed for keyword density; natural language required).
