# AutoMinder Closed-Beta Tester Checklist (15 minutes)
Thanks for testing. Do each step on your own phone. If anything is confusing, screenshot it and tell us what you *expected* to happen.

1. **Setup** — Install, open, add your real car (make/model/year + mileage). Did you finish without help? How long did it take?
2. **First verdict** — On the home screen, in one sentence: what is the app telling you about your car? (If you can't answer, that's a bug — tell us.)
3. **Notification** — Enable notifications. When a reminder arrives, use the buttons ON the notification (Done / Snooze 3 days / Update mileage) without opening the app. Did they work?
4. **Log a service** — Record an oil change (any date/cost). After saving: can you tell what changed in your plan?
5. **Interruption** — Start adding a second vehicle, then switch to another app mid-form, then come back. Is your typing still there?
6. **Export** — Settings → export your data (CSV). Did you get a file you could open/share?
7. **Accessibility (pick one)** — Turn on dark theme, OR set font size to largest, OR enable TalkBack for 2 minutes. Does the home screen still make sense? Anything unreadable or unlabeled?
8. **Process recovery** — Restart your phone. Do reminders still work afterward?
9. **Honesty check** — Was anything the app told you wrong about your car? (Wrong due date, wrong mileage math, wrong status color?)
10. **The question that matters** — Would you use this before your next real repair visit? Why or why not?

Send back: answers 1–10, your phone model + Android version, and any screenshots.
