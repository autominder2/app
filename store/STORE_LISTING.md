## Full Description

Stop guessing when your next oil change is due. AutoMinder tracks every service, reminds you before anything goes overdue, and logs your fuel efficiency — all without needing an internet connection.

Whether it's an oil change, tire rotation, registration renewal, or brake inspection, AutoMinder keeps your car care on schedule so you can focus on the road.

🔔 SMART MAINTENANCE REMINDERS
• Set reminders by date, mileage, or both — whichever comes first
• Get notified before services become overdue — never get caught off guard
• Track oil changes, tire rotations, brake pads, filters, belts, and more
• Snooze non-critical reminders without losing sight of urgent ones
• 30-day advance alerts for registration and insurance renewals

⛽ FUEL LOG & MILEAGE TRACKER
• Log every fill-up in seconds with cost, volume, and odometer reading
• Track fuel efficiency trends over time to spot problems early
• Monitor cost per kilometer/mile to manage your car budget
• Intelligent fuel predictions that improve with every entry

🚗 MULTI-VEHICLE SUPPORT
• Manage your entire household fleet — cars, trucks, motorcycles
• Each vehicle gets its own service history, fuel log, and reminders

★ WHY AUTOMINDER? ★

Unlike other vehicle maintenance apps, AutoMinder was built with a clear philosophy:

✓ OFFLINE-FIRST — Your data stays on your device. No account required. No cloud sync. No subscriptions to access your own records. Works everywhere, even with no signal.

✓ SMART URGENCY SYSTEM — Other apps show you a flat list. AutoMinder prioritizes what actually matters. Overdue services always surface first and cannot be dismissed. The UI physically changes shape to communicate urgency at a glance.

✓ ZERO SETUP FRICTION — Add a vehicle, set a reminder, done. No registration walls, no email verification, no tutorials. Open the app and start tracking in under 30 seconds.

✓ LIGHTWEIGHT & FAST — No bloated features you will never use. AutoMinder launches instantly, uses minimal storage, and never drains your battery with background syncing.

✓ PRIVACY RESPECTED — No analytics harvesting. No ad tracking profiles. No data sold. Your maintenance schedule is nobody's business but yours.
