## Full Description

Stop guessing when your next oil change is due. AutoMinder tracks every service, reminds you before anything goes overdue, and logs your fuel efficiency — all without needing an internet connection.

Whether it's an oil change, tire rotation, registration renewal, or brake inspection, AutoMinder keeps your car care on schedule so you can focus on the road.

🔔 SMART MAINTENANCE REMINDERS

★ WHY AUTOMINDER? ★

Unlike other vehicle maintenance apps, AutoMinder was built with a clear philosophy:

✓ OFFLINE-FIRST — Your data stays on your device. No account required. No cloud sync. No subscriptions to access your own records. Works everywhere, even with no signal.

✓ SMART URGENCY SYSTEM — Other apps show you a flat list. AutoMinder prioritizes what actually matters. Overdue services always surface first and cannot be dismissed, and every status is carried by icon, label, colour and an edge rail together — never by colour alone.

✓ ZERO SETUP FRICTION — Add a vehicle, set a reminder, done. No registration walls, no email verification, no tutorials. Open the app and start tracking in under 30 seconds.

✓ LIGHTWEIGHT & FAST — No bloated features you will never use. AutoMinder is small, starts fast, and has no cloud sync draining your battery. The only background work is a periodic check for reminders that have come due.

✓ YOUR RECORDS STAY YOURS — Your vehicles, service history, fuel logs and notes are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us. No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync. We never sell your data.

For transparency: the app does send anonymized crash and performance diagnostics and anonymized usage events (such as "vehicle added") so we can fix faults and see which features matter. Ads in the free version use your device advertising ID, and in the EEA and UK we ask your consent before showing personalized ads. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

## Data Safety Form Answers

Derived from the MERGED release manifest and the actual SDK set, not from the
source manifest. Verified 2026-08-26. The previous version of this section
declared "app activity" as NOT collected while Firebase Analytics logs 29 event
types, and omitted crash/performance diagnostics and Sentry altogether — a
false Data Safety declaration is grounds for removal, not just rejection.

### Data collected AND shared

| Category | Data type | Purpose | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device or other IDs | Advertising ID | Advertising or marketing | No (free tier) |

Shared with Google (AdMob). `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` is present
in the merged manifest, so the answer to "does your app use an advertising ID"
is **YES**. Do not answer no.

### Data collected, NOT shared

| Category | Data type | Purpose | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|
| App activity | App interactions | Analytics | No |
| App info and performance | Crash logs | Analytics, App functionality | No |
| App info and performance | Diagnostics | Analytics, App functionality | No |

Sources: Firebase Analytics (29 `logEvent` call sites), Firebase Crashlytics,
Firebase Performance Monitoring, and Sentry (a second error/performance
reporter — easy to forget, and it has a live DSN).

### Data NOT collected
Name, email address, user IDs, phone number, physical address, location (precise
or approximate), photos or videos, files or documents, contacts, calendar,
financial or payment info, health data, messages, web browsing history, installed
apps. Vehicle records — VIN, plate, odometer, service history, fuel logs, notes —
never leave the device and are therefore not "collected" for Data Safety purposes.

### Security practices
- **Encrypted in transit:** Yes. All SDK traffic is HTTPS; the app declares no
  cleartext traffic and has no network client of its own.
- **Data deletion:** Yes, users can request deletion. Settings has a
  "Delete all data" action that erases every vehicle and all five related tables
  via CASCADE. Uninstalling also removes everything. The advertising ID is reset
  or deleted from Android's own settings.
- **Independent security review:** No.
- **Committed to Play Families policy:** Not applicable.

### Other declarations
- **Designed for children / target audience:** No — 18+. The app uses an
  advertising ID and serves personalized ads on consent, so it must not be
  declared as designed for children.
- **Contains ads:** Yes.
- **In-app purchases:** Yes — subscriptions and a one-time purchase.
- **Account creation:** No. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so the
  in-app account-deletion requirement does not apply.

## App Access Declaration (App content page)

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no gated regions, so no credentials
exist to supply. Declare this explicitly rather than leaving the section blank —
a blank access declaration invites a reviewer to request credentials that do not
exist, which is itself a rejection.

Recommended wording:

> All functionality is available without any login, account or special access.
> Open the app, complete the short onboarding, add a vehicle, and every feature
> is reachable. Paid ("Pro") features can be exercised with a Play licence
> tester account on the internal or closed testing track.
