# Privacy Policy for Milevora

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026  

Abdul Ghani ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Milevora mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

By using Milevora, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Milevora is designed with privacy at its core. Every record you enter stays on your device. The App transmits a limited amount of diagnostic, analytics, and advertising data described below.

### Information You Provide (Stored 100% Locally)

All data you enter into Milevora, including vehicle details, maintenance reminders, fuel logs, mileage readings, and service history, is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information.

### Information Collected Automatically

The App does not collect information that personally identifies you. It transmits the following non-personally identifiable diagnostic and telemetry data to third-party SDK providers:

- **Advertising Identifier**: Google AdMob, our advertising provider, may collect your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) to serve advertisements within the free tier of the App. This identifier is used solely for ad personalization and measurement purposes.
- **Ad Interaction Data**: AdMob may collect information about your interactions with advertisements displayed in the App, such as impressions and clicks.
- **Crash and Error Reports**: Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry collect anonymized stack traces and device information (model, operating system version) when the App encounters an error.
- **Usage Events**: Google Analytics for Firebase collects anonymized interaction events describing how the App is used (e.g. "vehicle added").
- **Performance Traces**: Firebase Performance Monitoring and Sentry collect anonymized startup and screen rendering metrics.
- **Purchase State**: Google Play Billing reports whether you hold an active subscription or one-time purchase. Payment details are handled securely by Google Play and are never sent to us.

**Screen contents are never transmitted.** Screenshot and view-hierarchy capture are explicitly disabled in our telemetry configurations to guarantee vehicle VINs, license plates, and private notes never leave your device.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- No names, email addresses, or account credentials
- No location data or GPS coordinates
- No contacts, photo libraries, or local device files
- No web browsing history or search queries
- No financial, banking, or credit card numbers

## 2. How We Use Information

### Your Local Data

All vehicle, maintenance, and fuel data you enter remains on your device. We use this data solely to provide App functionality, including displaying reminders, calculating fuel efficiency, and tracking service history. This data is never transmitted to any external server.

### Advertising & Diagnostic Data

The advertising and diagnostic identifiers are used to:

- Display relevant advertisements within the free tier of the App
- Measure advertising effectiveness and prevent ad fraud
- Identify, diagnose, and fix software bugs, ANRs, and crashes
- Monitor app performance and responsiveness across Android device models

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

All App data (vehicles, reminders, fuel logs, service records) is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database protected by standard Android operating system user sandboxing (UID/GID isolation).

### No Cloud Account Dependency

Milevora does not require account creation, cloud passwords, or third-party social logins. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage without a device backup, your locally stored information will be permanently deleted.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Milevora is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is designed for adult users of driving age (18 and older).

## 6. Your Rights

### For European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Residents (GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of advertising data. You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in `Settings > Google > Ads` on your device.

### For California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. The advertising identifier shared with AdMob is used solely for advertising and fraud prevention.
