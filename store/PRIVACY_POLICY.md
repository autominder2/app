# Privacy Policy for AutoMinder

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

Abdul Ghani ("we," "us," or "our") operates the AutoMinder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

By using AutoMinder, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

AutoMinder is designed with privacy at its core. Every record you enter stays on your device. The App does send a limited amount of diagnostic, analytics and advertising data, described below.

### Information You Provide

All data you enter into AutoMinder, including vehicle details, maintenance reminders, fuel logs, mileage readings, and service history, is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information.

### Information Collected Automatically

The App does not automatically collect information that identifies you personally. It does send the following to the third-party services listed in section 3:

- **Advertising Identifier**: Google AdMob, our advertising provider, may collect your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) to serve advertisements within the App. This identifier is used solely for ad personalization and measurement purposes.
- **Ad Interaction Data**: AdMob may collect information about your interactions with advertisements displayed in the App, such as impressions and clicks.
- **Crash and Error Reports**: Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry collect anonymized stack traces and device information (model, operating system version) when the App crashes or hits an error.
- **Usage Events**: Google Analytics for Firebase collects anonymized events describing how the App is used, such as "vehicle added".
- **Performance Traces**: Firebase Performance Monitoring and Sentry collect anonymized timings — app start-up, screen rendering, screen transitions and user interactions.
- **Purchase State**: Google Play Billing reports whether you hold an active subscription or one-time purchase. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are never sent to us.

Screen contents are not transmitted. Screenshot capture is disabled in both crash reporters, so a VIN, licence plate or note you have entered is not included in a report.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- No names, email addresses, or account credentials
- No location data or GPS information
- No contacts, photos, or files from your device
- No browsing history or search queries
- No analytics or usage telemetry
- No crash reports or diagnostics

## 2. How We Use Information

### Your Local Data

All vehicle, maintenance, and fuel data you enter remains on your device. We use this data solely to provide App functionality, including displaying reminders, calculating fuel efficiency, and tracking service history. This data is never transmitted to any server.

### Advertising Data

The advertising identifier collected by AdMob is used to:

- Display relevant advertisements within the App
- Measure advertising effectiveness
- Limit the frequency of ad displays
- Comply with ad fraud prevention requirements

## 3. Third-Party Services

AutoMinder uses the following third-party services:

### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

AdMob may collect:

- Android Advertising ID
- Device information (device model, operating system version)
- IP address (for approximate geographic location to serve region-appropriate ads)
- Ad interaction data

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

- Open your device Settings
- Navigate to Privacy (or Google > Ads)
- Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or "Delete advertising ID")

When you opt out, AdMob will serve non-personalized advertisements instead.

### Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Performance Monitoring

Operated by Google LLC and subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Used for crash reporting, anonymized feature-usage analytics, and performance monitoring.

### Sentry

Operated by Functional Software, Inc. and subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy (https://sentry.io/privacy/). Used for error monitoring and performance tracing alongside Crashlytics.

### Google Play Billing

Operated by Google LLC. Processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. Google Play handles payment entirely; we never receive or store payment details. The App records only whether an entitlement is currently active, on your device. You can view or cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store.

We use no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services other than those named above.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

All App data (vehicles, reminders, fuel logs, service records) is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database. This data exists only on your device and is protected by your device's own security measures (lock screen, encryption).

### No Cloud Sync

AutoMinder does not offer cloud backup, account synchronization, or any form of data transmission to external servers. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, your locally stored information will be permanently deleted.

### Data Security

While we do not transmit or store your data on external servers, we recommend that you:

- Keep your device's operating system updated
- Use a secure lock screen on your device
- Regularly back up your device using your preferred backup method

## 5. Children's Privacy

AutoMinder is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is designed for adult users of driving age (18 and older).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App, please note that no personal data is collected by us. Any locally stored data can be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings.

## 6. Your Rights

### For European Economic Area (EEA) Residents (GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA, you have the following rights regarding data processed by our advertising partner:

- **Right of Access**: Request information about what data is processed
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of data processing
- **Right to Object**: Object to data processing, including for personalized advertising
- **Right to Data Portability**: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise your rights regarding advertising data, you may:

1. Reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device settings
2. Opt out of ad personalization in your device privacy settings
3. Contact Google directly regarding data collected through AdMob

Since all other App data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over it at all times.

### For California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information is collected and how it is used
- **Delete** personal information held about you
- **Opt-Out** of the sale or sharing of personal information
- **Non-Discrimination** for exercising your privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information. The advertising identifier shared with AdMob is used solely for advertising purposes. You may opt out by disabling ad personalization in your device settings.
