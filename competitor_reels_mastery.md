### **3. HOOK MASTERY: THE HUMANIZED FORMULA**
*Based on analysis of world-class scriptwriters (e.g., Alex Hormozi, Steven Bartlett).*

**The "Anti-AI" Guardrail:**
- ❌ **Avoid:** "Unleash your potential," "Embark on a journey," "The secret to..."
- ✅ **Use:** "I don't care about...", "I was wrong about...", "Stop doing [X] if you want [Y]."

**The "Negative Hook" Strategy:**
80% of viral reels start with a negative or "Counter-Intuitive" statement. It triggers the brain's "Danger/Curiosity" sensor.
*Example:* "You aren't an alcoholic" vs. "How to stop drinking."
