AutoMinder helps you keep track of vehicle maintenance, mileage, fuel, and service records without turning your car into a guessing game.

See what needs attention, what is coming up, and what you have already logged - all from an offline-first app built for everyday drivers.

MAINTENANCE REMINDERS
Set reminders by date, odometer, or both. AutoMinder shows overdue and upcoming work clearly, including the timing or mileage behind each reminder.

SERVICE HISTORY
Log oil changes, tire rotations, brake work, inspections, repairs, and custom services. Keep a searchable record of what was done, when it happened, where it was done, and what it cost.

FUEL AND COST TRACKING
Record fill-ups, fuel volume, odometer readings, and spending. Review fuel economy and cost trends based on the entries you add.

MULTIPLE VEHICLES
Track maintenance for more than one car from the same app. Each vehicle keeps its own reminders, mileage, fuel entries, and service history.

PRIVACY-MINDED BY DEFAULT
No account is required for core tracking. Your vehicle records are stored on your device, with export and backup tools available from inside the app.

KEY FEATURES
- Date and odometer maintenance reminders
- Overdue, due soon, snoozed, completed, and unknown states
- Service history with costs, notes, workshops, and odometer readings
- Fuel log with spending and efficiency trends
- Multiple vehicle support
- Home screen widget for quick maintenance status
- CSV service-history sharing
- Dark mode and Material Design
- Offline-first core tracking
- Optional Pro upgrade for premium features and an ad-free experience

AutoMinder is for drivers who want a clear maintenance record, not another pile of paper receipts.
