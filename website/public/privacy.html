Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Abdul Ghani ("we," "us," or "our") operates the AutoMinder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

By using AutoMinder, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

AutoMinder is designed with privacy at its core. Your vehicle records, maintenance logs, and personal data are stored exclusively on your device. We collect only the minimum telemetry necessary to ensure app stability and serve advertisements.

Information You Provide

All data you enter into AutoMinder—including vehicle details, maintenance reminders, fuel logs, mileage readings, and service history—is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information.

Information Collected Automatically

We use third-party services to improve app stability and support development through advertising:

  • Advertising Identifier: Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID for ad personalization and measurement.
  • Stability & Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymized stack traces and device information (model, OS version) when the app crashes to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage Telemetry: Google Analytics for Firebase collects anonymized data about how the app is used (e.g., "vehicle added" event) to help us improve features.
  • Performance: Firebase Performance Monitoring collects anonymized app start-up times, screen rendering times, and network request timings to help us find slow paths.
  • Error Monitoring: Sentry collects anonymized error reports, stack traces, device information, and performance traces of screen transitions and user interactions (for example "opened vehicle detail"), to help us diagnose faults. Screenshots and on-screen content are not sent.
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing processes any subscription or one-time purchase you make. Google, not us, handles your payment details; we never see or store a card number. The App stores only whether an active entitlement exists, on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • No location data or GPS information
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device (photos added to vehicles remain on-device)
  • No browsing history or search queries

2. How We Use Information

Your Local Data

All vehicle data remains on your device. We use this data solely to provide App functionality, such as reminders and fuel efficiency calculations. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Diagnostic and Usage Data

We use crash reports and usage events to identify and fix technical issues and to understand which features are most valuable to our users. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

Advertising Data

The advertising identifier is used by AdMob to serve relevant ads and measure effectiveness. In the EEA, we gather your explicit consent before serving personalized ads via the Google UMP framework.

3. Third-Party Services

AutoMinder uses the following third-party services. Each is operated by the company named, under that company’s own privacy policy:

Google AdMob

Used to display advertisements. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics & Performance Monitoring

Operated by Google LLC. Used for stability monitoring and feature usage analysis. This data does not personally identify you. AutoMinder does not currently offer an in-app switch for diagnostics; you can limit ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings at any time.

Sentry

Operated by Functional Software, Inc. Used for error monitoring and performance tracing, alongside Crashlytics. Subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy. Screenshot capture is disabled, so the contents of your screens — including any VIN, licence plate or note you have entered — are not transmitted. AutoMinder does not currently offer an in-app switch for error monitoring.

Google Play Billing

Operated by Google LLC. Processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive or store your payment details. AutoMinder stores only whether a purchase is currently active, on your device, so paid features keep working offline. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can view or cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All App data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database within the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. AutoMinder does not add its own layer of database encryption; the data is protected by your device's own security — your lock screen and the file-level encryption Android applies to app storage. If you back up your device, these records are included in that backup and are then covered by your backup provider's protections.

No Cloud Sync

AutoMinder does not offer cloud backup. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, your locally stored information will be permanently deleted. We recommend using your OS-level backup (Google Drive backup for Android) to preserve data across devices.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoMinder is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete all information by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. You can manage ad personalization and analytics preferences within the device or app settings.
