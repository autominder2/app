Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Abdul Ghani ("we," "us," or "our") operates the AutoMinder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

By using AutoMinder, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

AutoMinder is designed with privacy at its core. Your vehicle records, maintenance logs, and personal data are stored exclusively on your device. We collect only the minimum telemetry necessary to ensure app stability and serve advertisements.

Information You Provide

All data you enter into AutoMinder—including vehicle details, maintenance reminders, fuel logs, mileage readings, and service history—is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information.

Information Collected Automatically

We use third-party services to improve app stability and support development through advertising:

  • Advertising Identifier: Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID for ad personalization and measurement.
  • Stability & Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymized stack traces and device information (model, OS version) when the app crashes to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage Telemetry: Google Analytics for Firebase collects anonymized data about how the app is used (e.g., "vehicle added" event) to help us improve features.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • No location data or GPS information
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device (photos added to vehicles remain on-device)
  • No browsing history or search queries

2. How We Use Information

Your Local Data

All vehicle data remains on your device. We use this data solely to provide App functionality, such as reminders and fuel efficiency calculations. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Diagnostic and Usage Data

We use crash reports and usage events to identify and fix technical issues and to understand which features are most valuable to our users. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

Advertising Data

The advertising identifier is used by AdMob to serve relevant ads and measure effectiveness. In the EEA, we gather your explicit consent before serving personalized ads via the Google UMP framework.

3. Third-Party Services

AutoMinder uses the following services from Google LLC:

Google AdMob

Used to display advertisements. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics & Google Analytics

Used for stability monitoring and feature usage analysis. This data does not personally identify you. You can opt-out of analytics via the App's settings.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All App data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted-at-rest SQLite database (where supported by the OS). This data is protected by your device's security measures.

No Cloud Sync

AutoMinder does not offer cloud backup. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, your locally stored information will be permanently deleted. We recommend using your OS-level backup (Google Drive backup for Android) to preserve data across devices.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoMinder is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete all information by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. You can manage ad personalization and analytics preferences within the device or app settings.
