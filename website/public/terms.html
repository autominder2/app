1. What Milevora Is

Milevora is a record-keeping and reminder tool. You enter your vehicle's details, service history, fuel purchases and odometer readings, and the App organises that information and reminds you when a service you have scheduled is approaching or overdue.

The App stores your records on your device. It does not require an account, and it works without an internet connection.
