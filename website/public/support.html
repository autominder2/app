Frequently Asked Questions

Is AutoMinder really free?

Yes! AutoMinder is completely free to download and use. The app is supported by non-intrusive advertisements. All core features — reminders, fuel tracking, service history, multi-vehicle support — are available at no cost.

Does AutoMinder need an internet connection?

No. AutoMinder works 100% offline. All your data is stored locally on your device. The only internet usage is for displaying advertisements, which is optional and doesn't affect core functionality.

How do I back up my data?

Since AutoMinder stores data locally on your device, your data is included in your device's standard backup (Google Backup). You can also export your service history as a CSV file from any vehicle's detail screen for your own records.

Can I transfer data to a new phone?

If you use Google's device transfer tool when setting up a new phone, your AutoMinder data should transfer automatically as part of app data migration. We recommend exporting your service history CSV as an additional backup before switching devices.

How do reminders work?

You can set reminders by date, mileage, or both. AutoMinder checks your reminders periodically and sends notifications when services are approaching or overdue. You can customize the reminder intervals for each service type.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Make sure notifications are enabled in both AutoMinder's settings and your device's system settings. On some devices, battery optimization may delay or block notifications. Try adding AutoMinder to your battery optimization whitelist:

  1. Open device Settings
  2. Go to Apps > AutoMinder
  3. Select Battery
  4. Choose "Unrestricted" or "Not optimized"

How many vehicles can I add?

You can add and manage multiple vehicles with no limit. Each vehicle gets its own independent set of reminders, fuel log, and service history.

What units does AutoMinder support?

AutoMinder supports both metric (kilometers, liters) and imperial (miles, gallons) units. You can change your preferred unit system in Settings at any time, and all displays will update accordingly.

How do I delete my data?

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Delete a vehicle: Open the vehicle, tap Edit, then Archive
  • Delete all data: Uninstall the app or clear app data in your device settings
  • Reset advertising ID: Go to device Settings > Privacy > Ads

The app crashed or is behaving unexpectedly

Try these steps:

  1. Force close AutoMinder and reopen it
  2. Make sure you're running the latest version from Google Play
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the issue persists, email us at autominder2@gmail.com with your device model and Android version
